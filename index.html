Straight answers

Repair or replace?

The question every homeowner asks. Here's how we help you decide, and we'll always tell you if a repair will do.

A repair usually works Lower cost

  • The leak or damage is limited to one area
  • Your roof is still well within its expected lifespan
  • A few shingles blew off, but the rest lie flat and intact
  • The problem is flashing around a chimney, vent or skylight

Time to replace Long-term fix

  • Leaks keep coming back in different spots
  • Shingles are curling, cracking or shedding granules
  • The roof deck feels soft or looks like it's sagging
  • Moss, daylight in the attic, or widespread storm damage

Not sure which side you're on? That's exactly what a free inspection is for.

FAQ

Good questions.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most homes are done in one to two days, depending on size, pitch and weather. We'll give you a schedule with your estimate and keep you posted if rain moves things.

Will my insurance cover storm damage?

It depends on your policy and the cause of the damage. We'll inspect and photograph everything, give you a detailed report, and can meet your adjuster on site so nothing gets missed.

Do I need to be home during the work?

No. We just need access to the outside of the house and an outlet. Most homeowners go about their day; it's loud, so pets usually appreciate a quiet room.

What warranty do I get?

Your materials carry the manufacturer's warranty, and our workmanship guarantee covers the installation. We'll give you both in writing when the job is done.
